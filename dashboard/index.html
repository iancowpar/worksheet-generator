The operating mechanism underneath an AI adoption program. Three views, one running system: where the program lives, what actually shifted, what it’s worth. Numbers are illustrative, drawn from the shape of an AI capability layer deployed across twenty product managers.

02 / Behavior change

What actually shifted.

Adoption measured by what stopped, not what launched. Workflows retired. Time-on-task collapsed. The fallback principle held.

Workflows retired
8

Manual workflows that no longer happen the old way for the PMs who adopted them. Counted only when extinction is sustained >30 days.

Tuesday Test pass rate
4 / 5

Of five workflows surveyed, four show sustained behavior change. One is still partial — some PMs still doing it manually.

Fallback compliance
100%

If the AI fails, the work still ships. No critical-path dependency on a single LLM call.
