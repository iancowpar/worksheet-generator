## ▸ MEASURE
*WHEN — "how do you measure adoption / behavior change"*

**trap → extinction → mechanism → lived proof → recognition close**

- **Trap:** usage same shape whether curious or integrated — can't tell adoption from theater
- **Extinction:** not "do they use it," *did they stop the manual version* — **"you don't have adoption, you just have addition"**
- **Mechanism:** don't split evenly → revert to familiar under load → highest-leverage move isn't training/champions, it's **removing the fallback** — *politically harder than programs, which is why functions default to programs*
- **Lived proof [story register]:** post-merger Slack push · Teams stayed outside eng · even willing users like me ran both **[BEAT]** *"that fallback is the structural problem — not the people, not the training"*
- **Close:** *"I read this job as the measurement model and the sunset design, not the training calendar"*

**Pause before the punchline. Don't rush the most important sentence.**

## ▸ GET-IT
*WHEN — "that's easy to say / how do you actually get that data, it's not in a dashboard"*

1. **Baseline first** — capture 4 wks *before* rollout; most orgs skip → can't measure what changed
2. **Voluntary creation** — custom slash commands / saved prompts / shared repos; nobody invests effort in a tool they don't trust
3. **Extinction** — manual reports deleted · meetings cancelled · legacy-workflow time plotted *down*
4. **Middle-out** — instrument at **team level** (individuals noisy · org theater)
5. **One qual question** — "what would you stop doing tomorrow if this vanished?" — 1:1 interviews + observation, not survey blast

Lagging ROI = too late → instrument leading. Clarity study: **45% can't measure ROI.**
