## What a recruiter screen at this level actually is

Different shape than an HM exploratory. Lisa Martin is:
- The gatekeeper to Lisa Rabideau
- Qualifying you (basic fit, comp, timeline, work authorization, level)
- Selling you on the role/company
- Probably running across multiple Deltek roles — she has visibility you don't
- Maybe already aware of the Lynette/CoS path through Nick

What she needs to confirm in 30 minutes:
- You're a real candidate (not a tire-kicker)
- Comp expectations are in band
- You can articulate why this role, why Deltek
- You'd represent well to the HM
- Whether to fast-track you into the active pipeline despite ongoing interviews

What you want from her:
- Real picture of the AI Enablement role (scope, internal vs external, who you'd serve)
- Comp band
- Process timeline
- Whether the AI Enablement and CoS paths should be coordinated
- Her read on Lisa Rabideau and what specifically she's hiring for

## The Lynette / CoS conversation — flag this early

Lisa Martin probably has visibility into multiple Deltek roles. Lynette's introduction is in motion on the CoS path. **Don't make her find out from someone else.** Mention it early, frame as transparency:

> *"One thing I want to flag up front, because you may already know — Lynette Kenney introduced me to the team through my friend Nick. After our conversation she suggested I might also be a fit for the Chief of Staff role to the CPTO and asked me to update my resume to align with that framing. So there's a parallel conversation in motion. I wanted to flag that so we can coordinate rather than have it become awkward later. The AI enablement role is the one I originally applied for and what drew me to Deltek; I'd want to understand the work on this side before assuming the CoS path is the right one."*

This does three things:
1. Transparent — she'll respect it
2. Signals you're a real candidate someone is advocating for internally (Lynette's a VP)
3. Gives her permission to help coordinate the two paths

She may already know. She may not. Either way, you've handled it.

## Questions to ask Lisa (pick 4-5)

In rough order of value:

1. **"What in my profile prompted you to pull me in mid-pipeline?"** Highest-leverage question. Lets her tell you what she sees in you — which tells you what to lean into.
2. **"What does AI enablement actually mean at Deltek? Is the role customer-facing, internal-facing, or both?"** Critical because the JD title is generic.
3. **"How would you describe Lisa Rabideau's leadership style and what she's specifically looking for in this hire?"** Recruiters at this level have real intel on the HM. This question gets you that intel.
4. **"What kinds of profiles have you been seeing in the active interviews? What's been missing?"** Lets her describe the gap your profile fills.
5. **"What's the process from here? Timeline, rounds, who I'd meet?"** Practical, signals you're thinking about it seriously.
6. **"Given the parallel CoS conversation through Lynette, do you have a view on whether these conversations should coordinate or stay separate?"** Only ask if you've raised the Lynette piece earlier in the conversation.

Don't run a checklist. Use the first two as openers, let her talk, follow threads.

## What to avoid

- **Hard pitching.** Recruiter screens reward composure, not aggression.
- **Walking through resume bullets.** She has the resume; doesn't need a recap.
- **Anchoring low on comp.** $180-200K is your honest range based on what we worked out for Suffolk. Don't let the conversation drift below that.
- **Confidential trash-talking about UKG.** RIF answer is clean; don't editorialize.
- **Acting surprised about anything she shares.** Listen forward.
- **Forgetting it's a two-way conversation.** Ask real questions; don't just submit to the screen.

---

## Tone

Direct, confident, curious. She's pulling you in despite an active pipeline — that's a real signal she sees something. Show up like a senior operator having a real conversation, not a candidate being evaluated.
