That is a build-from-zero, cross-1,500-person, 3-country people-leadership,

## Where to press Martin specifically (in order of leverage)

1. **The leveling question — press hardest here.** Not "I want more money" —
   *"is this leveled correctly for the scope the HM described?"* Make Martin
   carry that question up the chain. It's HM-sourced, so it's not you being
   difficult — it's a real mismatch she now has to reconcile. This is the only
   press that can move you out of the band entirely.
2. **$160K base as the floor of the conversation, not the ceiling to bargain
   down from.** Anchor it to the scope, not to your last number. Do not pitch
   below top-of-band; the scope justifies the ceiling as the starting point.
3. **Sign-on $20–25K — her cleanest lever, point her at it.** It sits *outside*
   the band, so it doesn't fight Roper comp discipline. Explicitly frame it as
   the no-equity bridge: *"equity is VP+ only here, so base and sign-on have to
   carry the whole story."* This is the ask Martin can most easily say yes to —
   make it easy for her to win.
4. **Written 6-month review if the band is hard-capped.** If she says the band
   can't move: *"then let's put a written 6-month comp review on the table,
   tied to either level or a bonus structure."* Keeps the band intact for her
   while committing to where you land.
5. **The CoS alternative — structural, not a threat, last.** Only if she signals
   level *and* band genuinely cannot move: *"if this seat can't be leveled to
   the scope, the honest question might be whether the Chief of Staff path
   Lynette flagged is the better level fit for what I'd bring."* Name it once,
   calmly, then stop. It's real leverage precisely because you don't brandish it.
