# Panel updated synthesis — May 28 (post-research)

Updated read on the Deltek panel after fresh research surfaced
substantial new context — most of it material to how to position the
case studies and the leveling argument.

## The five things that change the read

### 1. Bob Hughes (CEO from UKG) is the quiet ace

Bob Hughes became Deltek CEO **April 2024**. He came from **UKG**,
where he was **Chief Customer &amp; Strategy Officer**. Before that, ex-
Blackbaud, ex-Kronos. He overlaps your UKG tenure (you joined June
2024) by months.

Practical implications:
- Hughes-era Deltek is going to feel UKG-flavored in a way no other
  exec there will be. Same scale of enterprise B2B SaaS, same
  workforce/operations DNA, similar customer-experience emphasis.
- Your two case studies are *both* set at UKG. That makes them
  culturally legible to the CEO without translation. Tushaus and
  Rabideau will likely be aware of the UKG context already.
- Don't lead with this. Don't name-drop UKG-Hughes overlap in the
  room. But if asked about why Deltek specifically, the honest answer
  now has another layer: *"The company is heading in a direction I
  recognize from where I just came from."*

### 2. Deltek ALREADY has an AI Council and a public Responsible AI program

Major prep adjustment. Deltek runs a structured governance program:
- **AI Council** with Legal, IT, Product, Security, Compliance
- **Product Feature Review Committee**
- Public three principles: **Fairness, Transparency, Accountability /
  Accuracy**
- Public commitment that customer data is not used to train LLMs
- Foundation called **DNA (Deltek Native Architecture)**

What this changes:
- **Do not pitch building the AI Council.** They have one. Anything
  that sounds like "I would establish governance" reads as either
  unaware or aspirational about something that's done.
- **Do pitch operating inside the existing framework.** *"I'd be
  joining a program that already has the governance scaffolding —
  AI Council, Responsible AI principles, DNA foundation. The work
  I'd bring is the day-to-day adoption mechanics, the measurement
  rigor, the champion network that makes the framework actually
  operate."*
- Rabideau and Tushaus both helped shape that governance posture.
  Pitching to build it would land badly. Pitching to operate inside it
  reads as someone who did the research.

### 3. The role sits inside a built-out internal AI org

Deltek is currently hiring multiple senior AI roles:
- **GenAI Program Leader**
- **Principal, Enterprise AI Change &amp; Enablement**
- **Senior Principal, AI Transformation Lead**

Your Senior Manager role is one node in that bigger org.

What this changes:
- You will not be a single point of failure for Deltek's AI story
  (this was one of Tushaus's concerns from the earlier StoryBrand
  read). Good — but also reframes your value proposition.
- Your story shifts from *"I built it alone at UKG, and I'd build it
  alone here too"* to *"I built it alone at UKG with no formal
  scope, and the multiplier of bringing that experience into a
  function that already has formal scope and senior partners around
  me is what I'm excited about."*
- The "tenacious, build-from-zero" angle stays valid, but the *future*
  framing is now collaborative within a built-out org, not solo
  pioneer.

### 4. Tushaus's external pitch has hardened — agentic platform / vertical AI

Tushaus has done a LOT publicly in the past 12 months:
- **LaunchPod (Aug 2025)** — *"Agentic AI for Vertical Software"* —
  this is now his loudest external thesis
- **ProjectCon 2025 (Nov)** — led Vantagepoint kickoff session on AI

Plus Deltek's platform-level rebrand at ProjectCon 2025 — "**systems

Agentic Financial Close Q3 2026). The language is harder around
**agentic**, **orchestration**, **vertical AI**.

What this changes:
- Your embedded Claude Code function as a routing layer maps even
  more cleanly to *vertical agentic AI orchestration* than it did
  six months ago. That story is now exactly the kind of work Deltek
  is publicly committing to.
- Don't crib "systems of reason" — that's their phrasing, you'd
  sound like you read the press release on the way in. But operate
  in the same belief layer.
- Tushaus will probe harder than the earlier dossier suggested. His

### 5. Karen Alden + two rounds of layoffs = real cost discipline

Confirmed by Agent 1: **Karen Alden is VP HR**, likely the HRBP for

Context she's operating in:

- Roper Technologies ownership (acquired 2016 for $2.8B) — margin

But also — they're **hiring senior into AI**. The Sr Manager role sits
alongside Principal, Sr Principal, and GenAI Program Leader reqs. This

where Deltek is allocating budget.

What this changes for the leveling argument:
- The leveling pitch has to land in Karen's vocabulary, not just yours.
- Translated: *"This role's scope is bigger than the title implies, and

- The discipline: don't push leveling at the panel itself (way too
  early). But the case studies should be built to make the case
  *implicitly*. When Karen sees the debrief, she should think *"this
  candidate operates at a higher level than the role is titled

## Updated through-line for the panel

Three lines that now work better than the previous frame:

1. **"You already have the governance scaffolding. The piece I'd
   bring is the operating mechanics that make it real on a Tuesday."**
   This positions you precisely where the org has a gap. Don't say
   "Tuesday" out loud — but the Tuesday Test thesis IS what they
   need, told in their language.

2. **"My case studies are both behavior-change and risk-instrumentation
   built inside the same enterprise SaaS environment that Bob came
   from."** Don't say the second half. But the implicit alignment is
   real, and it makes the work culturally legible without translation.

3. **"The investment Deltek is making in this function is significant
   — and what makes that investment pay back faster is having
   someone who's already lived the muscle, not someone who has to
   build it from scratch in front of you."** Subtle leveling
   argument made through value framing.

## What NOT to do given the updated context

- **Don't pitch Hughes-overlap explicitly.** It's a tailwind, not a
  card to play.
- **Don't crib "systems of reason."** Operate in the belief, not the
  phrasing.
- **Don't reference ProjectCon as current.** It's Deltek Elevate now.
- **Don't dismiss the cost-discipline reality.** Karen reads ROI
  language. The case studies should land in dollars-and-behavior
  language, not in vision language.
- **Don't tell Tushaus what agentic AI is.** Show him the agentic
  AI you've shipped. He's been the public voice on this for a year.
