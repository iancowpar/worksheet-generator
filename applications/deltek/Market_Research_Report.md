**Glassdoor "AI Program Manager" US median: $171,799** (25th–75th $143,607–$209,853).

**Robert Half 2026 Tech Salary Guide:** AI roles getting +4.1% bumps in 2026, the only category outpacing the 1.6% tech baseline.

**Assessment:** $165K base sits at ~25th percentile. $175K is fair-to-conservative (40-50th percentile) given scope. Aggressive would be $185-195K or Director title. BlackRock and Atlassian at $200K+ suggest Deltek is paying mid-market discount, consistent with Roper's disciplined comp reputation.

## 2. Deltek onboarding — why employees praise it

Specifics that recur:
- Welcome emails sent 3 days before start to ensure equipment/access ready
- Two days of structured HR/orientation training, then manager-led warm welcome
- Peer mentor/buddy assignment
- New Hire Learning Path in Deltek's Talent Management platform, integrated with class schedules

Quote: *"smooth and organized... onboarding team went above and beyond to ensure they had everything needed before start date"*

**Caveat:** HR/welcome layer is excellent; role-specific 30/60/90 is uneven. Shadow-a-peer training "didn't teach the product." Deep role learning is "on the fly." That gap is precisely what Ian's role exists to close for P&T.

## 4. Home office / remote stipend

**Weak spot.** No formal home office stipend surfaced. Glassdoor reviews explicitly: *"lots of perks for at-office staff, but next to nothing for remote workers"* and *"home office covered costs"* on employees' wishlist. Only adjacent perk: 10% Verizon discount.

Industry norm for mid-market SaaS 2026 is $500-$1,500 one-time setup + ~$50/mo internet. **Deltek appears below norm. Worth asking for as a negotiation lever.**

## 8. Non-base negotiation levers

If $175K is the cap:

- **Sign-on bonus:** $10-50K typical for Sr Mgr; 5-20% of base. Sign-ons come from a different budget than base — easier yes. **Aim $15-25K.**
- **Accelerated comp review at 6 months** with predefined criteria (tied to onboarding program launch milestones)
- **Higher target bonus %** (15% vs 10%) — modest base impact, real upside
- **Home office stipend** (one-time $1-2K) and/or monthly internet
- **Confirmed MacBook + dual monitor + headset** before start
- **PTO accrual credit** to match current employer's balance
- **Conference / professional development budget** earmarked annually ($3-5K) — easier sell given role is enablement
- **ESPP enrollment confirmation** and clarity on July 2026 plan upgrade eligibility
- **Title path commitment** (e.g., Director eligibility at 12-18 months on performance)

## 9. Title benchmarking

"Senior Manager, AI Adoption & Enablement" is **underleveled vs scope** for the comp band at peer companies:

- Apple uses near-identical title — but Apple Sr Mgr ≈ Director elsewhere
- NBCUniversal, BlackRock, Landing Point all use **Director** for this scope ($150-275K)
- Deltek itself has **"Director, AI Program Leader"** and **"Sr Director, AI Solutions Architect"** roles posted internally — so Director title exists
- Owning a 30/60/90 across a 1,500-person org, building champion networks, defining measurement models, and managing 2-3 reports including international staff = **Director scope at most peer companies**

Title compresses comp ceiling and external mobility. **Strong lever to push on** — either Director title or written path to it.

## 10. Encouraging signals, concerning signals, verification questions

**Encouraging signals:**
- 87% recommend overall; 4.5 culture, 4.6 work-life balance pre-2026 leadership shift
- Onboarding praised — Ian inherits a strong base
- Project Nation half-Friday genuinely valued
- Roper ESPP 2026 upgrade is real money (~$4K+/yr risk-free)
- The role exists because the gap is acknowledged — political will to fund it

**Concerning signals:**
- **Annual January RIFs are the norm under Roper.** Even strong performers cite being cut. P&T org is not exempt.
- **Culture shift narrative** — multiple Sr Mgr reviewers describe a pivot to cost-cutting; the 5pt YoY recommend drop is meaningful
- **Global async load:** 12-14 hour days reported for roles bridging Philippines/India. Ian's role is structurally exposed to this.
- **No equity, no LTI, no sabbatical, weak remote stipend, no wellness budget** — the "boring SaaS subsidiary" benefits profile
- **Title underlevels scope** — affects next-role mobility
- **Engineering "significantly understaffed"** comments raise question of whether AI adoption mandate has sufficient partner capacity

**Verification questions before signing:**
1. What was P&T headcount impact in Jan 2026 RIF? Is the AI team protected/funded through FY26 plan?
2. Does the role get a Director path? Written timeline?
3. ESPP eligibility confirmed, including July 2026 plan upgrade?
4. MacBook + monitor + headset standard? Home office stipend?
5. Bonus payout history — has Deltek hit 100% target in last 3 years?
6. PTO: 19 days vs the "unlimited PTO" some reviewers cite — which tier applies?
7. Async expectation: hard cap on early-morning/late-night meetings, or open-ended?
8. Sign-on bonus available to offset no equity?

**Assessment on $175K push:** Justified by market data, not aggressive. Pair it with sign-on ($15-25K), confirmed equipment, ESPP enrollment, and a written 12-month Director review. If base is truly capped at $165K, the sign-on + accelerated review combo recovers most of the gap and is typically easier to approve.
