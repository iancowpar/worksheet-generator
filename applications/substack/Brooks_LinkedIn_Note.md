# LinkedIn connection note to Alison Wood Brooks

The framework-literate move: the note itself should pass her own test.
She studies conversation; she will read a connection note as conversation.
Almost all cold author outreach is a boomerask (gesture = admiration,
substance = "notice me / here's my thing"). A note clean of that stands
out most to the one person tuned to catch it.

## TALK applied to a 300-character connection note

- **T — Topic:** one specific idea, named precisely (boomerasking) and what
  it did. Specificity is the proof of real engagement, like a follow-up
  question. Generic praise proves nothing.
- **A — Asking:** don't. A question in a cold note obligates a reply —
  boomerask-shaped. Asking belongs in live reciprocal conversation; save it
  for if she accepts and a thread opens.
- **L — Levity:** one light touch. Warm, not stiff.
- **K — Kindness:** complete as a gift. Low-demand. She owes nothing.

## The test

If the note would feel incomplete to you without a reply, it's a boomerask.
Send only a note you'd be genuinely glad to send into silence.

## Leave out (hard)

The essay. The Substack. "Would love to connect / chat." Anything that asks
for her attention. If she replies and engages, share the essay then —
reciprocal, not extractive.
