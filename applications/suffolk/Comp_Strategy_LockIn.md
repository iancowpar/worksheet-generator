When we talked I didn't know it was direct people management with two reports, no bonus or equity in the structure, or the 4-day-a-week in-office expectation. ## If Joe pushes back on base

Pivot to total package:

> *"If $200K base is constrained, I'd want to talk about a sign-on bonus to bridge — somewhere in the $15-25K range — plus a comp review at six months tied to performance. That structure works for both of us."*

Sign-ons don't sit on the band the same way base does. Joe has more flexibility there.

## Sequencing

## Risk assessment

Real but small. Worst case: Joe says "we can't get there" and you settle around $190K. The asymmetry favors anchoring high — Lead PM negotiations don't break over $15-20K gaps when both sides want the deal. They break when comp expectations are fundamentally misaligned.
