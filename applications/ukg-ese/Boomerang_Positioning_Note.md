## Why the boomerang is an asset here specifically — not a liability

ESE builds internal tooling for UKG's own teams. **You already did exactly this
inside UKG** — the migration tool, the AI capability layer, the five-panel
analytics product, the sprint instrumentation were all internal tooling /

internal users (PMs, engineering, Services), the stakeholders, the products, the
migration program, and the actual tools ESE's roadmap touches. Ramp time is
near zero — and for an internal-product org, ramp time *is* the cost.

tooling inside UKG for two years — Services-facing migration tooling, an org-wide

## The departure — the honest, non-bitter frame

Factual, forward, said once, never re-litigated:

> *"I was part of the April 2026 reduction in force — a role elimination, not a
> performance exit. I left on good terms and I'd come back to the right ESE seat
> without hesitation; the work there is the work I most want to be doing."*

Rules:
- **Never** frame it as grievance, surprise, or unfairness. A RIF is structural;
  treat it as structural.
- **Don't volunteer it** before it's asked, but **don't dodge** — recruiters see
  the dates and the prior employment immediately. Pre-empting reads as confident;
  getting caught reads as evasive (same discipline as the Rabideau gap pre-empts).
- Leave-on-good-terms is doing real work here — internal references are your
  strongest channel (below). Anything bitter poisons that well.

The last line matters. A boomerang who reads as "nothing else worked" is weak;

strong. You have the live pipeline to make that true — use it.

## Channel discipline (Envestnet warm-path rules apply)

- **Dara and Leah are warm channels — use them.** An internal referral or a warm
  intro to the hiring manager should land **before or with** the ATS profile,
  not after. A cold profile that auto-screens before the warm path activates
  turns a referral into "overturn a rejection."
- **Line up internal references now**, while goodwill is fresh: former
  manager(s), the two PMs you coached on the AI layer, Services-Consultant
  partners from the migration tool, anyone who'd say "we'd take him back." For an
  internal org, a current-employee vouch outweighs the resume.
- New candidate profile: **same name, same email, same identity.** Do not create
  distance from the prior tenure — the prior tenure is the entire pitch. The
  profile is a re-application, not a reset.
