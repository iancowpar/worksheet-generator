## What it is (general)

- Formerly **FinancialForce**, rebranded to Certinia in early 2023.
- A services-as-a-business platform **built natively on the Salesforce
  platform** — uses Salesforce's data model, security, infrastructure;
  custom objects and apps on top. Lives inside Salesforce.
- Core product for UKG's use case: **PS Cloud (Professional Services
  Automation)** — project management, resource management, time/expense,
  billing, invoicing for services delivery.
- Other products UKG may or may not use: ERP (financials, rev rec),
  Customer Success Cloud, Services-CPQ. Not surfaced publicly.

## Likely UKG use case (confirmed enough to plan around)

The "CX" customer for this PO role = internal services teams that
