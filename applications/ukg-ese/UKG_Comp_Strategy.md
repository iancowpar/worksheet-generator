### UKG-specific RSU caveat
UKG is privately held (Hellman & Friedman / Blackstone post-merger).
RSUs are real comp but are **private-company equity** — illiquid until a
tender or liquidity event. Specifically ask the recruiter:
- Initial grant size (number of units + current internal valuation)
- Vesting schedule
- What happens on departure (cliff treatment, accelerated vesting on
  liquidity event, etc.)
- Recent tender / recap history (when was the last liquidity event;
  what was the valuation move)

**The RSU conversation is more important than $5-10K of base haggle here.**
The base band is tight ($45K range); the RSU grant has more room to move.

### Boomerang reality — cuts both ways
- **In favor:** Tasos has stated preference for UKG-impacted candidates;
  Dara pulling at TA Director chair; first to apply; near-zero ramp
  (UTA program ownership + UKG Salesforce data fluency + cross-org
  network already in place).
- **Against:** Boomerangs often get anchored to exit comp + small
  adjustment as the path of least resistance. Exit was **$130K base +
  10% bonus = $143K cash**. Without active counter-anchoring, the
  default offer comes in at $130K base "matching where you were" with a
  soft RSU add. That's a discount on real value here.

### Negotiation targets
- **Anchor: $145-147K base** (top or near-top of band)
- **Floor: $138K** — modest defensible premium over exit
- **Walk-away: $130K base** — matching exit. Below this, the boomerang
  math literally doesn't work; this becomes a comp regression.
- **RSU grant: meaningful initial allocation + vesting clarity** —
  treat as the primary lever
- **Bonus target %: confirm 10%** (or push higher if structure allows)
- **Sign-on: ask, expect resistance.** UKG typically structures through
  bonus / RSU rather than sign-on.

### Total Y1 target at full anchor

- ~$15K bonus

### How to frame it without burning the warm channel
The comp conversation lives with TA. Frame as **scope-and-leverage,
not entitlement**:

> "Given the scope here, the institutional knowledge I'd walk in with,
> and the comparable comp I'm seeing across the other live processes,
> here's where I'd be looking."

Don't mention specific competing companies. Don't name specific
Deltek/Suffolk numbers. **Let the existence of pipeline do the work
without naming.**

**Use the HM preference as counter-anchor:** Tasos explicitly values
the UKG-impacted profile. *"You specifically value the zero-ramp
UKG-impacted profile — let's price that in"* is a defensible move,
not entitled.
