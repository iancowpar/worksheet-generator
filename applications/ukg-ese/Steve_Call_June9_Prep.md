# Steve Marshall call prep — late afternoon, June 9

Triggered by: Deltek offering at top of band ($165K + 10%) with formal
offer expected Monday. UKG needs to compress. Steve already pinged
coordinators for panel scheduling and wants to connect today.

## The shape of this call

Steve is now actively advocating for you inside UKG. He needs:
1. Clarity on what would actually close you
2. Confidence that you're decidable, not shopping
3. Ammunition to push on his side for comp / leveling / timing

You need:
1. Panel logistics confirmed (date, time, who, format)
2. Realistic timeline from panel to decision
3. Honest signal on whether UKG can be comp-competitive
4. Read on Atul / Matt parallel options
5. Steve's coaching on what you need to do to win

## Opening

Open warmly. Don't lead with the offer — Steve already knows. Lead
with appreciation and partnership.

> "Steve, thanks for moving so fast on the coordinator side. I want to
> use this call to give you the picture from my side, hear what you're
> seeing from yours, and figure out what we both need to do to make
> this work."

That's the tone. Partners working a problem.

## Questions in priority order

### Tier 1 — must ask

1. **"What's the panel logistics looking like? Has Tasos confirmed tomorrow, or is it slipping?"**
   - If tomorrow: lock it. Confirm time, format, length, who's on it.
   - If later in the week: ask what's driving the delay and whether
     it's recoverable.

2. **"Who's on the panel and what do they each care about? Anything
   specific I should be prepared for?"**
   - Steve has the inside view. Pump him for the panel-shaped intel.
     Names, roles, hot buttons.

3. **"Realistic case — if the panel goes well, what's the path to a
   decision? Days from panel to verbal yes?"**
   - The concrete number you need. *"Days, not weeks"* is the right
     pressure.

4. **"Deltek is offering $165K base plus 10% bonus. UKG's band tops at
   $147K. If UKG wants to be competitive, what's the path? Top of band
   plus RSUs plus sign-on, or does the role need to be re-leveled?"**
   - This is the comp positioning question. Steve can either answer or
     route it. Either is useful.

### Tier 2 — ask if time

5. **"Does this timing change whether the Lead PM role under Atul or
   the Matt Alliances role becomes worth exploring? Dara had been
   positioning for those — are they still live, or has this PO
   process foreclosed them?"**
   - The Atul question is the highest-upside path. Steve will know.

6. **"What's your read on the candidate field? You mentioned three.
   Is the internal candidate already preferred, or is this genuinely
   open?"**
   - Steve may not answer directly but his tone will tell you.

7. **"What did Tasos's feedback actually say beyond the Salesforce
   concern? Dara passed the headline but I want to make sure I'm not
   missing anything before the panel."**
   - Last calibration before panel.

### Tier 3 — close on partnership

8. **"What do you need from me to advocate effectively on your side?
   Anything I should be sending Tasos, anything I should be saying
   differently in the panel?"**
   - The partnership-closer. Recruiters love this question.

9. **"Is there a version of this where UKG can verbally indicate
   intent before Deltek's offer window closes, even if the formal
   offer takes longer? That would let me make a complete decision."**
   - The "verbal intent" play. UKG can sometimes signal "we want you,
     paperwork's coming" faster than formal offer.

## What to be ready to discuss when Steve asks

### "Why UKG over Deltek if comp is lower?"

Honest version:
> "Three things. The team and the org I already know — that's a real
> ramp advantage and a real fit advantage. The trajectory if the Atul
> role opens or this PO seat evolves the way Tasos suggested. And
> family — Lowell-based life is the version of me I want to be."

Don't perform. That's the honest answer.

### "What would close you?"

Specific version:
> "To make the math work, I'd need to be at or near $150K base with
> standard RSU grant and bonus structure, ideally at a Senior PM title
> not a PO title. If UKG can't meet that, I understand — Deltek's
> offer is real and I'd take it without bitterness. But that's the
> shape that would make this the answer."

The "$150K + Senior PM title" anchor is your real ask. Don't soften
it. Don't inflate it.

### "Can you extend the Deltek window?"

> "Probably 10-14 days from Monday is the realistic ceiling. Anything
> beyond that and Deltek will lose patience or read it as me
> shopping. So roughly June 29 is the latest UKG can land."

That's the timeline he needs to operate against.

### "Is this offer real?"

If Steve probes — *"Are you sure Deltek is moving at $165K, or are
you negotiating that up?"* — be straight:
> "That's the verbal offer at top of band. Formal offer Monday. I'm
> not negotiating it up — they offered top of band."

Don't bluff. Steve will see through it and your credibility takes a
hit.

## What NOT to volunteer

- Deltek's start date options
- Anything about Preservica
- Anything about how the Deltek panel went specifically
- Your decision framework or weights

Keep the conversation focused on UKG and the things Steve can act on.

## Close

> "Steve, thanks for the partnership here. The honest version is I'd
> love this to be the answer. What I need from you is a fair shot at
> making it the answer — panel locked in tomorrow if possible, comp
> positioning honest, and a decision by [date]. I'll do my part on
> the panel. Tell me what else I can do."

Then stop talking. Let him work.

## Take notes on the call

Steve will surface a lot. Write down:
- Panel details (date, time, who)
- Comp positioning answer
- Timeline-to-decision number
- Anything specific Tasos said in feedback
- The Atul/Matt status

This is the call where UKG's path becomes legible. Capture it.
