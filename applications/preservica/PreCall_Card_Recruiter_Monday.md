## Anticipated recruiter questions

### "Tell me about yourself / why this role?"

60 seconds. Lead on regulated-industries plus AI builder. Anchor
language like: *"I keep ending up at the intersection of regulated
industries, AI, and product. A decade at Broadridge in financial
services where wrong outputs had regulatory consequence. Two years at
UKG building an AI capability layer from zero across twenty PMs. The
role at Preservica reads like the formalization of work I've already
been doing — applying AI responsibly inside a platform whose customers
take long-term consequence seriously."*

### "Why are you looking to leave UKG?"

Brief, forward-looking, no UKG-bashing. The honest version: you've
been building the AI capability layer as a side function without
formal scope or mandate. The Preservica role exists by design — Senior
PM with AI programmes ownership reporting to Head of Product. That's
what you want next.

### "What's your AI experience specifically?"

Don't recite a tools list. Tell two specific stories:
- The migration tool prototype in Claude Code → engineering delivery
  package → projected ~30% time reduction. Shows shipped, not demoed.
- The five-panel analytics product with the embedded Claude Code
  function as routing layer, cutting a defect list ~97%. Shows AI
  inside a product, not adjacent to it.

Then if there's room, the AI capability layer story (twenty PMs, no
mandate).

### "How do you think about AI responsibility / risk?"

This is the question to be most ready for, given the JD. Your real
answer: *"The pattern I keep seeing in enterprise AI is that
deployment gets called adoption. The honest measurement principle I
work to is 'extinction over addition' — count what workflows stopped,
not what tools launched. Responsibility starts at measurement. If you
can't tell honestly whether the AI is replacing work or just adding
to it, you can't govern it."*

Then reference the Starbucks story — the AI inventory tool retired
nine months in. The workers told them in February; the org acted in
May. The gap between knowing and acting is where responsibility
fails. That's the topic of the Tuesday Test essay you published this
week, if natural to mention.

### "What kind of role are you looking for?"

Senior IC with real ownership. Not management track. Reporting into
product leadership (Head of Product matches). Comp band aligned with
US Senior PM market.

### "What's your timeline?"

Realistic. You're actively in process at one or two other places
(don't name them by name). You'd want to be honest that this isn't a
12-week leisurely search — if Preservica wants to move, you can move.

### "What are your comp expectations?"

Band is known ($140–160K US base). The conversation now is about
total package, not base alone.

Pre-loaded answer: *"My base target frame is $160K+, which I know is
at the top of what you've shared. But I'd want to understand the full
package — equity, options or RSUs, bonus, benefits — before drawing
conclusions. If the total picture lands and the role is what it reads
as on paper, we have plenty to discuss."*

That answer:
- Doesn't apologize for caring about money
- Doesn't pretend the band isn't at your floor
- Doesn't anchor too high (you're at $160K not $180K)
- Opens the door to negotiating on total comp, not just base
- Signals you'll evaluate the whole picture before committing

### Three things to surface about total comp on the call

1. **Equity / RSUs / options.** *"What does the equity picture look
   like at Preservica? Options, RSUs, something else?"* UK-anchored
   companies vary widely on this. Preservica is private — equity could
   be options with real upside if they're scaling toward acquisition,
   or it could be nominal.
2. **Band flex for the right candidate.** Don't ask transactionally
   — ask conversationally: *"How firm is that band, and is there
   typically room when the candidate brings something distinctive?"*
   The answer tells you whether the $160K top is a hard cap or a
   guideline.
3. **Growth-stage equity exposure.** *"What's Preservica's stage right
   now? Where are you in the funding / growth arc?"* The phrase
   *"entering the next phase of platform evolution"* in the JD
   suggests a real growth moment. If they're approaching an exit,
   equity matters more. If they're stable mid-market, base is doing
   more of the work.

### "Can you work the UK hybrid?"

No. Remote US is the only path. Don't apologize. *"I'm US-based
(Massachusetts), so I'd be remote on the US side."* Simple.
