## What this materially changes

### The strategic frame for Preservica

Before: *"Lottery ticket on exit + real role + reasonable cash."*

Now: *"Real role + below-projection cash + zero equity upside even
if they exit."*

This doesn't kill Preservica as an option — the role substance is
real and Stuart Reid as exec sponsor means top cover. But it
fundamentally changes what you're trading for what:

- You're paying ~$35K/year (vs. projected mid) for the role substance
- You don't participate if Preservica exits at premium multiples in 2-4
  years
- Career capital is real (AI Programmes title, Stuart Reid sponsor,
  programmatic role) but it has to be the thing you want

### What still works in Preservica's favor

- The role itself reads as strong career capital — AI Programmes
  ownership, CPO sponsor, board visibility, real growth trajectory
- Mark's directness and the team's collaborative reputation suggest a
  decent culture fit
- Local Boston office presence — Methuen is a reasonable commute when
  needed
- The £50M target means meaningful work to do and visible outcomes
  available
- Mark moves fast — could close inside June

### What changes about negotiation strategy

The earlier playbook assumed three meaningful levers: base, equity,
sign-on. Two of three are now off the table:

- **Base at $160K** is confirmed as Mark's anchor. Asking for $165K
  is *possible* but unlikely to land — Mark's tone was "we're at top
  of band."
- **Equity is structurally unavailable.** Not a negotiation question.
- **The 3% bonus is company-wide policy.** Not negotiable individually.

**The remaining levers are softer and less material:**
- Sign-on bonus — worth asking, $5-10K plausible
- Title precision — could push "Senior PM, AI Programmes Lead" to
  signal the scope, though Mark may not have authority on title
- Start date / vacation rollover from current employer

**The new highest-EV move is probably elsewhere.** If Preservica
advances and you have an offer + Deltek or Teradyne in flight,
Preservica's offer becomes the leverage instrument for the others,
not the other way around.
