# What's behind this Preservica role — strategic thesis

The JD describes the work. This file describes the business reason
the hire exists. Useful for reading the offer, negotiating, and
deciding whether to take it.

## The single-sentence thesis

**The VCT sponsor needs a liquidity event. AI is the path to a premium
exit. Preservica has been talking about AI more than shipping it. The
hire is to make the AI story *real* — shipped, defensible,
customer-visible — in the next 12-24 months while the sponsor
positions for an exit.**

Everything else is downstream of that.

## The structural facts that converge on this thesis

1. **Sponsor pressure for exit is real and time-bound.** Gresham House
   Ventures (formerly Mobeus) has held Preservica since 2016 — ten
   years. Preservica is **27.5% of GHV's VCT portfolio value** —
   their single largest holding. VCT sponsors need liquidity events;
   GHV is overweight on this one asset. No public IPO/M&A activity
   yet. The math says they need a path to exit in the next 2-4 years
   max.

2. **Growth is solid but not exit-worthy without a new narrative.**
   FY25 revenue £15.8M, +15% YoY. That's healthy mid-market SaaS but
   not "premium-multiple" growth. To command an exit multiple that
   pays the sponsor back well, they need either accelerated growth or
   a strategic-acquirer narrative. AI delivers both:
   - Acquirers (Microsoft, IBM, Iron Mountain, OpenText, Box) pay
     premiums for vertical SaaS with credible AI capabilities they
     don't want to build themselves.
   - Public-market comps for AI-positioned SaaS are higher than
     non-AI peers.

3. **The Microsoft Copilot integration created the opening but
   exposed the gap.** Preservica launched Preserve365 + Microsoft
   Copilot integration. That's strategic — it inserts them into the
   Microsoft ecosystem. But Microsoft moves at Microsoft pace.
   Preservica needs to keep up. That requires someone who actually
   ships AI features.

4. **Internal signal that they know the gap exists.** Glassdoor
   review language from current/recent employees:
   *"Company desperately trying to grab onto AI's coat tails."* When
   employees say this out loud, the gap is real and management knows
   it. The hire closes it.

5. **Customer pressure is real and accelerating.** Preservica's
   customers (governments, archives, libraries, enterprises in
   regulated industries) are themselves under board and constituent
   pressure on AI. They're asking *"what's your AI story?"* in sales
   calls. The salespeople need a credible answer that points at
   shipped capability, not roadmap slides.

6. **Leadership team is in execution-mode.** CPO Stuart joined 2022
   (ex-Sony / Symantec / Kroll). January 2025 "leadership
   strengthening" hires in security and customer success. This is a
   company past the build phase and into the make-it-work phase.

7. **Role title literally says it.** "Senior PM, **AI Programmes**"
   — programmatic, not feature-PM. Plural. Board-visible. The title
   itself is the giveaway.

## What this means for Ian — strategic implications

### 1. The work is more important than the title suggests

This isn't a "let's see what happens" hire. It's tied to a corporate
trajectory with real exit pressure. That's leverage during negotiation
AND meaningful responsibility once the role starts. The person who
lands this role has the chance to materially shape Preservica's
2027-2028 trajectory.

### 2. Success criteria are clearer than the JD says

Reading through the business reason, the actual success criteria
are:
- **Visible AI shipping.** Real features customers can point to, not
  roadmap slides.
- **Adoption signals (internal AND customer).** Sales needs proof
  customers actually use the features they're being sold.
- **Defensible measurement.** Because the sponsor and any acquirer
  will diligence the AI claims. "Hours saved per customer," "% of
  customers using AI features," etc.
- **Responsible AI posture.** Because customers in regulated
  industries care, and acquirers want to inherit clean governance.

All four of these are Ian's wheelhouse. Zero-Translation Building +
extinction-over-addition + the Tuesday Test + composite measurement
work + Broadridge regulated background = an unusually clean fit for
the *actual* job, not just the JD version.

### 3. The timing window is finite

If the sponsor wants liquidity in 2-4 years, Ian's effective window
is **12-24 months** to make the AI story real before diligence
season. Two or three major capability rollouts. That's enough to do
meaningful work but not enough to take a slow ramp.

### 4. The personal upside is the equity question

If Preservica exits in the next 2-4 years at a premium AI multiple,
even modest options could be material. That's the lottery ticket
worth understanding more clearly on Monday — what's the equity
structure, what's the typical grant at this level, what's vesting.

### 5. The downside scenario is also real

If the AI story doesn't land in 12-18 months, the sponsor may pivot
to a fire-sale exit, and the AI Programmes role becomes a vulnerable
position in any integration. Mitigation:
- Negotiate severance terms (asking is reasonable for a role this
  tied to a corporate bet)
- Treat the role as a 24-month resume-building bet, not a 5-year
  career home

### 6. Why Ian fits the role's real shape — not just the JD shape

Most candidates Preservica considers will be "AI PMs." Ian is
something more specific that maps better to the actual business need:

- **Compliance-grade background** (Broadridge 10 years) → translates
  directly to Preservica's customer base
- **Zero-Translation Building + extinction-over-addition** → exactly
  the language acquirers want to hear in diligence
- **Substack thought leadership** → external credibility that
  Preservica's PMM team can ride
- **The AI capability layer shipped at UKG scale** → proof he can run
  programmatic AI adoption work, not just feature PM

This is the angle to play — *"I look like the AI PM you posted for,
but the more specific match is to what you actually need: someone
who can ship credible AI inside regulated-industry contexts and
defend the work to an acquirer's diligence team."*

Don't say it that bluntly. But it should inform the energy.

1. **"What does success look like for this role in 12 months?"**

   - The honesty of the answer tells you whether they have a real
     strategic thesis or are reactive.

   *"What's Preservica's stage right now — where are you on the
   growth/funding arc?"*
