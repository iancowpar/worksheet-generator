## The single most important insight to internalize first

**Helena's published management philosophy is "Giving Away Your Legos."**

She wrote a 2018 Medium essay (`@oohelen` on Medium) titled *"To
Managers 'Giving Away Legos' for the First Time."* Her thesis: mastery
is demonstrated by *teaching and handing off*, not by holding on.
Resisting delegation caps growth. The metaphor — every promotion is a
lego you give away.

**This is the lens through which she will read your AI capability
layer story.** Not "Ian built a thing." Not "Ian led a program."
*"Ian built something other people built on top of and ran without
him."* That's give-away-the-legos in product form.

The way you tell the UKG capability layer story for Helena
specifically:

> "It worked because it didn't depend on me. Twenty PMs picked it up,
> built their own slash commands, retired their own workflows. Three
> of them became champions and started onboarding the next wave
> without me having to ask. The honest measure of whether I built
> something good was whether it kept running when I wasn't running it."

That's the Legos arc. She'll recognize it as her own philosophy
applied in a different domain. Strong fit signal.

> want this US seat to own outright versus co-pilot with you — and
