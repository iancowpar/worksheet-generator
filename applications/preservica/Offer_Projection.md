# Preservica — projected offer structure + negotiation strategy

Research-grounded projection of what an offer would likely look like
if the process reaches that stage, plus the negotiation levers worth
pulling.

## The funding state (the anchor for everything else)

**Preservica is a VCT-backed UK private company.** Spun out of
Tessella 2012. HQ Abingdon UK + Boston office. Backed since 2016 by
**Gresham House Ventures** (formerly Mobeus Equity Partners).

Critical facts:
- Last reported funding round: **December 2021, $6.6M** growth round.
- **No 2024-2025 funding, acquisition, or IPO activity** — they
  haven't moved publicly in years.
- **FY2025 revenue: £15.8M, +15% YoY.** Mature, slow-but-steady
  growth-stage SaaS, not high-growth.
- Headcount ~142-144.
- **Preservica is Gresham House's largest single VCT holding at 27.5%
  of portfolio value** — meaning the sponsor has *strong* incentive to
  engineer a liquidity event in 2-4 years. But nothing public yet.

The sponsor pressure is the most interesting structural fact. They
need an exit. AI traction is the obvious path to one.

## The three negotiation levers worth pulling

### 1. Push base to $160K (top of band)

Bonus is a % of base — every dollar on base compounds. The argument
isn't transactional; it's strategic centrality:

> *"The role is titled 'AI Programmes' which signals board-level
> visibility, and your customer base is one where credibility on AI
> matters more than almost anywhere else. The base at top-of-band is
> appropriate to the seriousness of the bet, not a stretch."*

Glassdoor's "AI coat-tails" complaint and the comp/benefits 3.4
rating mean **they know they're undercompensating for the AI talent
they need right now.** Anchoring at $160K is defensible.

### 2. Convert equity uncertainty into a sign-on or guaranteed Y1 bonus

VCT-backed private UK companies rarely grant meaningful IC-level
equity. Exit timing is uncertain (last round Dec 2021, sponsor wants
liquidity in 2-4 years but nothing public yet). **Treat equity as
lottery-ticket upside, not core comp.**

The high-EV ask: **convert the equity uncertainty into cash
certainty.**

Two ways to do it:
- **Sign-on bonus $15-20K.** Frames as bridging to your asking range
  without breaking the band.
- **OR: guaranteed first-year bonus floor.** *"100% of target bonus
  regardless of company attainment for year one."* Hedges against the
  company performance gates that often eat into the actual payout.

Either ask is defensible. Don't ask for both — pick one.

### 3. Pin down US benefits before signing

UK companies often use a US PEO (Remote.com, Deel, Justworks). Quality
varies. Things to ask explicitly before signing:

- **401(k) match percentage.** If below 4%, ask them to top it up via
  base.
- **Healthcare premium coverage %.** US standard is 80-100% employer.
- **PTO days.** UK companies are often more generous (25+ days).
- **Equipment / home-office stipend.**
- **Which PEO they use** (affects benefit quality and admin).

These aren't sexy asks but they're easy wins. Total value of the
benefits stack can swing $10K+ depending on what they're actually
offering.

## What changes the projection materially

If any of these surface on the call or in the process, recalibrate:

  rather than 24-48). Pushes the EV of equity higher, may justify
  lower base.

  Principal AI Programmes" or similar). Could justify pushing above
  $160K base.

  Means they've internally accepted they need to pay more.

  swing total comp $10-15K and changes the calculus on base.
