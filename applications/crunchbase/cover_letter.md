The line in the job description I keep coming back to is "this role is about building community and building signal." Most community work I've watched from inside product orgs gets one of those two and abandons the other. The marketing-led version optimizes for engagement and produces no usable roadmap signal. The product-led version treats community as a focus group and never builds the trust that makes the signal worth listening to. The version that actually works is the one this role is reaching for—a community designed from the start to do both.

At UKG, I built a PM automation library that became the product org's shared AI capability layer. The interesting part wasn't the tooling; it was the adoption pattern. I designed it for organic spread rather than top-down rollout, and what came back through that informal community of internal users was sharper than any survey: which workflows were broken, where people were quietly working around the platform, what they wished existed. The build and the listening loop were the same loop. That's the muscle I'd bring to a customer-facing community at Crunchbase.

I'd also point to the live AI prototyping piece of this role. I've been using AI tools inside customer conversations at UKG—mocking up a workflow or feature concept in real time so the person on the other end of the call has something concrete to react to instead of a hypothetical. The conversation gets sharper, the feedback gets more honest, and I usually leave with a clearer read on what they actually want than a structured discovery session would have produced. That's a habit, not a one-off, and it maps directly onto what you're describing.

Outside work I publish The Unofficial Leader on Substack—weekly, to a practitioner audience, on AI adoption and the systems that change how teams work. It's the closest analog I have to running a community publicly, and the reader feedback loop has been one of the more useful inputs into my own product thinking.

Where I'd be honest about the gap: I haven't run a Discord, a Discourse instance, or a paid community platform end-to-end. I've run programs—Brand Champion regional lead and Business Innovation workstream lead for the Fire Up ERG at UKG—and built an audience on Substack, but I'd be picking up the specific platform mechanics on the way in rather than carrying them in.
