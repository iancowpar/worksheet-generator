# Decision framework — UKG / Preservica / Deltek

The discipline this enforces: criteria and weights set *before*
offers land, then offers scored against them. If the math
contradicts your gut, that tells you the weights are wrong OR your
gut is reacting to something the framework missed. Either way you
surface the disagreement honestly instead of retrofitting reasoning
to whichever offer shows up first.

Built for the three active live tracks. Updated as offers and signals
land.

## The dimensions and the scoring guidance

Ten dimensions. Each scored 1–10 per role when offers / interviews
surface enough info. Weights total 100, set BY YOU now, BEFORE
offers compare.

| # | Dimension | Definition / scoring guidance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Year-1 cash** | Base + likely bonus payout + sign-on. 10 = $200K+, 5 = $150K, 1 = $100K |
| 2 | **Equity upside** | Real expected value of options/RSUs over 3-year horizon. 10 = material liquid upside, 5 = modest, 1 = none |
| 3 | **Scope of ownership** | Defining a function vs. slotting in. 10 = first/defining role, 5 = clear seat with autonomy, 1 = backlog-running |
| 4 | **Career capital (24-mo story)** | What you can say in your next interview. 10 = transformative, 5 = solid Senior PM line, 1 = lateral move |
| 5 | **Strategic centrality** | How important is your role to the company's actual bets? 10 = board-level visibility, 5 = visible function, 1 = staff role |
| 6 | **Manager / team fit** | From interview signals. 10 = great working chemistry confirmed, 5 = professionally fine, 1 = real friction |
| 7 | **Company health / risk** | Growth trajectory, layoff risk, ownership stability. 10 = clear runway and growing, 5 = stable, 1 = real risk of disruption |
| 8 | **Day-to-day energy fit** | The work you actually do on Tuesday. 10 = naturally energizing, 5 = neutral, 1 = draining |
| 9 | **Optionality (doors opened)** | What this enables next. 10 = opens multiple distinct paths, 5 = keeps options open, 1 = narrows |
| 10 | **Geographic / lifestyle fit** | Commute, hybrid demands, time zone, travel. 10 = ideal, 5 = workable, 1 = strain |

## How to use this when offers land

1. Refresh the scores with the latest info from each process.
2. Look at the weighted totals.
3. **Ask yourself: does this match my gut?** If yes, you've validated
   both. If no, surface the gap honestly — either adjust weights (was
   I undervaluing X?) or note the gut is reacting to something the
   framework missed (and write that in).
4. Don't make the call from the scorecard alone. Use it as the
   discipline check on the call you're already leaning toward.

## Watch-outs to enforce regardless of score

- **No accepting in the room.** Every offer gets 24-48 hours minimum.
- **Don't commit to anything verbally before all three have played
  out.** *"This is a real offer and I'm taking it seriously — I have
  parallel processes I need to wrap before committing"* is your line.
- **The leveling conversation belongs at offer stage, not before.**
- **Don't optimize for the role that comes first** unless it's
  obviously the strongest. Real offers come with windows; you can
  usually negotiate 5-10 days to wait for parallel processes.

## What's NOT in this framework (deliberately)

- "Which company has the best brand" — vanity metric
- "Which has the most prestigious title" — pre-empts scope of work
- "Which feels safest" — risk is in the dimensions; "safe" is a vibe
- "What other people would do" — your weights, not theirs
